# Survey Summary

User feedback survey for the Event Discovery and Management Platform, collected on Microsoft Forms on 30 and 31 October 2026.

## About the responses

- 14 responses received and 12 used. Two test responses were excluded.
- 10 attendees. 5 people also answered as organisers (a promoter, two societies, an independent creator and one other), 3 of whom also attend events.
- Most respondents are young phone users reached through friends and social media. The results are indicative and not representative.
- The raw export is not stored in this repository until responses are done.

## Attendees (10)

- 6 often miss events they would have liked to attend, 3 sometimes and 1 rarely.
- They find events through word of mouth, WhatsApp and Instagram (9 each), TikTok (8) and posters (5).
- Most popular event types: Music and Nightlife (9), Networking (8), Markets and Food (6), Workshops and Learning (6), Arts and Culture (4), Sports and Fitness (3), Community and Charity (2).
- First thing they need to see: date and time (3), price (3), description (2), venue name (1), how to get there (1).
- If an event changes, 6 want a phone notification, and 1 each chose a site notification, SMS, email and WhatsApp.
- 7 would create an account, 1 only after finding 3 relevant events, and 2 would not.
- 8 mostly use a phone and 2 use both. Nobody uses only a laptop. Speed on limited data is extremely important to 6.

Ranking of features (average position, 1 is most useful):

| Feature | Average rank |
|---|---|
| Suggestions based on interests | 2.6 |
| Being told if an event is cancelled or changed | 2.6 |
| Reminders before an event | 3.5 |
| Adding to your calendar | 4.7 |
| Saving events | 4.7 |
| Seeing regular weekly sessions | 4.9 |
| Searching and filtering | 5.0 |

## Organisers (5)

- 3 run regular events. Nobody objected to listing a series once.
- All 5 accept admin approval, but 4 said only if it is quick. All 5 chose 24 hours as the longest wait.
- All 5 want to fix details themselves without a review.
- 4 said engagement statistics would be extremely useful and 1 somewhat useful.
- The hardest part of promoting is reaching new people (4), then tracking interest (1).
- Ticket sales: at the door (3), free events (2), bank transfer or WhatsApp (2), online ticket platform (1).
- Paying a small fee: 2 yes, 2 maybe, 1 no. The main worries were cost and not seeing results.

## What changed in the requirements (v0.3)

| Finding | Change |
|---|---|
| Price matters as much as date and time | Price required on every listing (BR-20, OR-02, UR-05) |
| Some people want to look before joining | Browsing needs no account (BR-18, UR-03) |
| Phone notifications preferred | Web push is a priority research item (UR-16, OQ-03) |
| Organisers expect a quick review | 24 hour review target (BR-19, OQ-14) |
| Organisers value statistics | Basic counters moved to Should Have (OR-16, SYS-09) |
| Word of mouth and WhatsApp are the main channels | Share button added (UR-18) |
| Most organisers sell at the door or are free | Ticket link can be a note on how to get tickets (OR-08) |
| Cost and results are the worries | Posting stays free for the MVP (OQ-02) |

## Not changed

- Recurring events stay Should Have (3 of 5 organisers run regular events).
- Editable details rule confirmed.
- Mobile first confirmed.
- Search and filter stays Must Have, even though it ranked last. It is likely seen as expected.

## Ideas for later

- Show how many people have saved an event.
- A leaderboard of popular organisers.
- A light and dark theme.

## Limitations

- Small sample from one social group.
- The ranking assumes the first item listed is the most useful.
- Two people picked an unlabelled "Option 5" as their organiser type.
- The first four attendees were also shown the organiser questions before the form routing was fixed.
